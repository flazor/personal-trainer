# Next Workout: Squat & Pull Focus — Post-Race (Tue 2026-05-19)

First gym session after the Saturday 2026-05-16 trail relay (16 km / 2000 ft vert). Expect residual quad DOMS from the descents regardless of how the race went — even strong races leave a 3–5 day quad shadow when descending volume is low in training. Squat & Pull is the right session to slot here because we can scale lower-body load by feel without abandoning the day's structure.

Wed 05-13 bench session was clean: 4×5 @ 45 kg, all pulls and core ✓. That clears the path for the post-race bench bump on Friday 2026-05-22 (47.5 kg). This session is squat + pull, so bench gets the natural rest the plan asks for.

**Load adjustment vs. the pre-race prescription**: squat opens at **45 kg** (one notch under the 47.5 kg that was on the line). If quads feel surprisingly fresh at the top of warm-ups, you can elect to do 47.5 kg — but the default is the conservative re-entry. Lunges and hip thrust scaled to favor glutes over quads.

## Warm-Up (12 min — longer than usual on purpose)
- 5 min light rowing or skierg (easy pace — assess how the legs feel)
- Couch stretch: 90s per side (extra hold — hip flexors will be tight after the race)
- Glute bridges: 2 x 10 (bodyweight, squeeze at top — wake the glutes back up)
- Leg swings (front/back and lateral): 10 each direction per leg
- Bodyweight squats: 2 x 10 — **gauge the quads here**. If these feel cooked, drop squat to 42.5 kg or skip squat entirely and substitute leg press / goblet squat
- Walking lunges: 1 x 10/leg bodyweight (re-pattern, gentle)
- Squat warm-up build: 1x10 @ 20 kg (bar), 1x5 @ 30 kg, 1x3 @ 40 kg
