# Next Workout: Bench Focus — Solo (Rep-Out Proxy for 1RM)

**No spotter today, so we're not doing a true 1RM.** Instead, the top set is a **rep-out (AMRAP)** at a submaximal weight with the **safety pins set just above your chest** in a power rack (or use a Smith machine, or a press machine if your gym lacks a power rack with bench). This gives a clean 1RM estimate without the failure-pin risk of a max single, gets you back into the groove after a 7-day layoff, and keeps tomorrow productive even without a partner.

You'll do the real 1RM test on a future Tuesday or Friday when a spotter is available. After today, run `/personal-trainer "ran the rep test, got X reps at 60 kg"` and I'll either schedule the spotted 1RM or move into the next block based on the estimate.

## Session

### A. Barbell Bench Press — Rep-Out Test

**Set the safety pins / J-hooks at chest height** (a fingertip below where the bar touches your chest at the bottom). If the bar pins, you can roll out and stand up safely. Do not bench tomorrow without this setup.

Build-up sets (rest 60–90s):

| Set | Reps | Weight | Notes |
|-----|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | 8 | 20 kg (bar) | Groove the path, set arch and foot drive |
| 2 | 5 | 30 kg | Same tempo |
| 3 | 3 | 45 kg | Get used to plates |
| 4 | 1 | 55 kg | Single — feel the bar speed before the test |

Top set (full rest, ~3 min):

| Set | Reps | Weight | Notes |
|-----|------|--------|-------|
| Test | AMRAP | 60 kg | Stop at **RPE 9 / 1 RIR** — leave 1 rep in the tank, do not grind to failure. Count clean reps only (no pin touch, no butt off bench). Expected range: 4–8 reps |

Back-off (rest 2 min):

| Set | Reps | Weight | Notes |
|-----|------|--------|-------|
| 2x5 | 5 | 50 kg | Volume back-off — RPE 7. Restoring training stimulus after deload + missed week |

**1RM estimate from the test set** (Epley formula): `1RM ≈ 60 × (1 + reps/30)`. Quick reference:

## Progression Notes
- **Why a rep-out instead of a single**: a true 1RM without a spotter is the textbook bad-idea bench scenario. AMRAP at 60 kg with safety pins set is safe — if you can't lock out, the bar lands on the pins. Epley estimate is within ~5% of true 1RM in the 4–8 rep range.
- **Why 60 kg for the test**: you hit 52.5 kg × 5 cleanly at RPE 8 in week 7. 60 kg is +14% load — should land you in the 4–8 rep zone, which is the sweet spot for a useful estimate.
- **Squat 40 kg** (down from 42.5): you've been deloaded then idle for a week. 2.5 kg backwards is a small concession to make the first set feel light; if it flies, ramp back up next session.
- **Hip thrust restart**: third attempt to get this back in. 20 kg bar-only, do not skip.
- **Plan is unchanged**: the 1RM test is still on the books, just deferred until you can grab a spotter on a Tuesday or Friday. The next training block keys off that test result.
