# Next Workout: Upper Body Pull + Squat

This is the new pull day. It fits perfectly after yesterday — that session was push + lower
with **no pulling at all**, so today balances him out. Pull-dominant for shoulder health, with
a **light** squat to groove the pattern. The squat stays easy on purpose: legs worked hard
yesterday and there are games this weekend. Lifted before practice — keep it crisp.

## Warm-Up (8-10 min)
- 5 min row or bike
- Band pull-aparts, scapular pull-ups / 10s dead hang, thoracic rotations
- Leg swings, hip circles, 10 bodyweight squats, then 1 light set of the goblet squat

## Session

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Weight | Rest | Notes |
|----------|------|------|--------|------|-------|
| Goblet Squat | 3 | 8 | 16 kg (est.) | 90s | Kept LIGHT today — legs trained yesterday. Just groove the pattern: chest tall, full depth, controlled |
| One-Arm Dumbbell Row | 3 | 10/side | 20 kg (est.) | 75s | Est. starting weight. Pull to the hip, squeeze the shoulder blade, no torso twist |
| Lat Pulldown *(or assisted chin-up)* | 3 | 8-10 | 35 kg (est.) | 75s | Est. starting weight. Full stretch at top, drive elbows down to the ribs |
| Face Pull | 3 | 15 | Light (est.) | 45s | Rear delts + cuff health. Pull to the forehead, elbows high. Light and controlled |
| Band External Rotation | 2 | 15/side | Light band | 45s | Throwing-arm cuff prehab. Elbow pinned to your side, slow |
| Biceps Curl | 3 | 10 | 12.5 kg | 60s | Same load as the curl/press combo yesterday. Strict, no swinging |
| Hanging Leg Raise *(optional)* | 2 | 10 | Bodyweight | 60s | Core; control the legs down |

## Progression Notes
- **First true pull session** — yesterday had bench, overhead press, and triceps but no rows or pulldowns, so this fills the gap and protects the shoulders.
- **Goblet squat held light (16 kg):** legs did trap bar + split squats yesterday and games are this weekend. Today is about the movement, not load — we'll load the squat properly on a fresh Thursday.
- **Rows, pulldown, face pulls are new** — all estimated starting weights. Pick loads where the last rep is clean. We'll set real working numbers from today's results.
- **Biceps curl at 12.5 kg** matches yesterday's curl/press combo.

## Cooldown (5 min)
- Lat stretch, cross-body shoulder stretch, quad/hip-flexor stretch (we squatted)
- Hold each 20-30s; light arm-care band work if the throwing shoulder feels tight
