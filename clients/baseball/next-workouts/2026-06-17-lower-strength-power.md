# Next Workout: Lower Body Strength & Power

First session of the new plan. Monday = no practice, so we push lower-body strength here.
Power work comes first while you're fresh. Leave 2-3 reps in reserve on every working set —
we build with good technique, not by maxing out.

## Warm-Up (8-10 min)
- 5 min easy bike or row
- Leg swings (front/back and side/side), hip circles, walking lunges, 10 deep bodyweight squats
- Knee-to-wall ankle mobility, then 2 sets of 5 low pogo hops to prime the legs

## Session

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Weight | Rest | Notes |
|----------|------|------|--------|------|-------|
| Box Jump (step down) | 4 | 3 | Bodyweight | 90s | Pick a box you clear easily. Soft, quiet landing — quality over height |
| Trap Bar Deadlift | 3 | 5 | 65 kg | 2-3min | Up from 60 kg×10 — fewer reps, a touch heavier for strength. Should feel like 2-3 left in the tank |
| Bulgarian Split Squat (DB) | 3 | 8/leg | 15 kg each hand | 90s | Same DBs as last time, now strict 8/leg. Control the way down, knee over toe |
| Romanian Deadlift (barbell) | 3 | 8 | 40 kg | 90s | Est. starting weight — new lift. Hinge at the hips, soft knees, feel the hamstring stretch. Keep it light to learn it |
| Pallof Press (cable/band) | 3 | 10/side | Light (est.) | 60s | Est. — pick a load you can hold steady. Resist the twist, don't let your torso rotate |
| Standing Calf Raise *(optional)* | 2 | 12 | Bodyweight | 60s | Full range, pause at the top |

## Progression Notes
- **Trap bar 60→65 kg:** last session was 3×10 at 60 kg with reps to spare. Dropping to 5 reps and adding a little weight starts the strength build. If 65×5 feels easy across all 3 sets, we go up next Monday.
- **Bulgarian split squat held at 15 kg:** last time was 3×10; tightening to strict 8/leg with full control before adding load.
- **RDL and Pallof are new** — both start light on purpose to groove the movement. Better to nail the hinge at 40 kg than to chase weight.
- **Box jumps** are for power, not fatigue — full recovery between sets, no rush.

## Cooldown (5 min)
- Easy walk 2-3 min to bring the heart rate down
- Static stretch (hold 20-30s each): hip flexors, hamstrings, quads, calves
